## Added results

- A rank-two null projection in signature \(11+3\) gives a residual
  \(9+1\) space. The explicit torus reduction has no mixed metric
  and matter Hessian at the zero-field vacuum. A positive tensor
  exchange has the opposite sign from the required hardening,
  and finite polynomial quadratic blocks cannot give the saturating
  entire multiplier.
- A symmetric Fock-space channel gives the Gaussian-cell multiplier
  as a unitary matrix coefficient. The total shift converges absolutely
  for almost every frozen gas. Its full real spectral support excludes
  any semibounded realization of the same shift generator.
- The Gaussian cluster-sector elimination has a definite sign.
  With a fixed bare light kinetic term, a positive heavy sector
  cannot produce the proposed ultraviolet suppression by that exchange.
- The averaged inverse moments in the power-law Poisson model are
  entire and zero-free, with a quantified asymptotic remainder.
- The routing argument covers all hard subspaces, including
  disconnected support and one-loop hard regions of multi-loop graphs.
  The one-loop background tuning is distinguished from the forest
  and BRST hypotheses for all-order physical amplitudes.
- Two positive-time curvature packets give an explicit negative
  reflected norm. A positive spectral mixture obeys a sharp
  two-point lower bound on its approximation error.
- A quartic interaction of linearized curvature in a bounded
  window has a non-Gaussian cutoff-free Euclidean law, with a rate of
  convergence and convergence of all finite curvature moments.
  The negative reflected norm persists at small coupling.

The finite-window interaction is a law of linearized curvature.
A completion with the proposed free curvature covariance cannot use
reflection-positive Euclidean measures converging to that limit.
