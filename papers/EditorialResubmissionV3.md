# Editorial resubmission note for version 3

Version 3 reorganizes the brane-clustering proposal around explicit tests of its ultraviolet and positivity claims. It retains the intersection complex, curvature-coupled fields, and geometric applications from version 2. A new null-projection calculation distinguishes the \(11+3\) worldvolume from the \(27+3\) ambient signature. An explicit four-dimensional reduction then shows why the local curvature couplings examined here cannot yield the proposed scattering multiplier. The revision constructs that multiplier as a unitary matrix coefficient, proves absolute convergence of the proper-time shifts for a frozen Poisson gas, and gives a gauge-invariant curvature observable with negative reflected norm. It also derives a cutoff-free curvature interaction in a bounded window and specifies the scope of the perturbative one-loop cancellation. The manuscript has been reformatted in AMS article style and the bibliography checked. The original author order and corresponding author are retained, and Ushashi Bhattacharya is added as the last author for her contributions to the Poisson brane-gas analysis.
